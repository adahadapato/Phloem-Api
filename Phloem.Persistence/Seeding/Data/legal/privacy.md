# Privacy Policy

> **Draft – to be reviewed.** This is a temporary wording while Phloem's full privacy policy is prepared. Words in [square brackets] will be filled in.

This policy explains what personal data Phloem collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and your rights. Phloem is run by [Company name], [registered address] ("Phloem", "we", "us"), which is responsible for your data. Contact: **support@phloem.app**.

## 1. What we collect {#what}

- **Account:** name, email, password (stored only in a scrambled form we can't read), phone number, country, profile photo, and whether you turned on two-step sign-in.
- **For selling and parcels:** date of birth and home address (to check you are 18 or over).
- **Orders:** what you ordered, from which shop, prices, delivery address and time, payment status.
- **Parcels:** sender and recipient names, phone numbers and address, what is inside and its declared value, weights, prices and the parcel's journey.
- **Sellers:** shop details, business email and phone, dish photos and descriptions, documents you upload (business registration, food hygiene certificate, photo ID), balance and payouts.
- **Technical:** sign-in records, and what your browser or phone keeps for you (see section 6).

We do not collect your full card details: payments are handled by our payment provider.

## 2. Why we use it {#why}

- To run your account and sign you in safely.
- To take, deliver, refund and support your orders and parcels.
- To check sellers and senders (age, documents) and keep Phloem safe from fraud.
- To send you messages about your orders, parcels and account, and (only if you agree in Notifications) offers and new dishes.
- To meet legal duties such as tax, accounting and customs.

## 3. Who sees what {#who}

- **Sellers** see your name, delivery address and what you ordered from them, only for their own orders.
- **Couriers and the parcel hub** see what they need to deliver.
- **Customs authorities** may receive parcel details when the law requires.
- **Service providers** that help run Phloem (hosting, email, payments) process data for us under contract.
- **Public tracking** shows only a parcel's progress: never names, phone numbers, addresses, contents or prices.
- Seller documents are private: only Phloem's team can open them.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## 4. Where it is kept {#where}

Your data is stored on servers run by our hosting provider [location to be confirmed]. When data moves between the UK, Ghana and Nigeria, we protect it as the law of each country requires.

## 5. How long we keep it {#how-long}

- Your account data: while your account is open. When you delete it, we remove or anonymise it, except what we must keep.
- Orders, payments and parcels: [6 years] for tax and accounting.
- Dish photos on past orders are kept so old orders still show what was bought.
- Seller documents: while the shop is open and for [period to be confirmed] after.

## 6. Cookies and storage {#storage}

The website keeps a few things in your browser so it works: your sign-in, your basket and the country you picked. We do not use advertising cookies. The app keeps the same on your phone.

## 7. Your rights {#rights}

Depending on where you live (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 in the UK; the Data Protection Act 2012 in Ghana; the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023), you can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, object to or limit how we use it, and withdraw consent (for example to marketing). Email **support@phloem.app**. You can also complain to your data protection authority: the ICO (UK), the Data Protection Commission (Ghana) or the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## 8. Children {#children}

Phloem is not meant for children. You must be 18 or over to sell or send parcels.
