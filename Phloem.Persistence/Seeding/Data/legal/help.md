# Help & support

Can't find your answer? Email **support@phloem.app** and include your order number or tracking number if you have one. We usually reply within one working day.

## Ordering food {#orders}

### How do I track my order?
Open [My orders](/orders) and tap the order. You'll see where it is: placed, paid, being prepared, on its way or delivered.

### I placed an order but didn't pay. Is it lost?
No. Unpaid orders stay in [My orders](/orders) with a **Pay now** button. They are always shown at today's prices, and any dish that has run out is left out when you pay.

### Can I cancel an order?
You can cancel an unpaid order at any time. A paid order can be cancelled within **30 minutes** of paying, as long as the shop hasn't started preparing it, and you get a full refund. See [Ordering food](/terms#buyers) in our Terms.

### When do I get a refund?
If you cancel in time, or the shop can't make your order, you get a full refund to the way you paid. It can take a few working days to show. See [Payments](/terms#payments).

### Something is wrong with my food
Email **support@phloem.app** within 24 hours with your order number and what went wrong. We'll look into it with the seller.

### I have an allergy
Dish descriptions come from sellers. Please check with the seller before ordering: Phloem can't guarantee a dish is free from allergens.

## Sending parcels {#parcels}

### What can I send?
The up-to-date list of items we can't carry, or carry only with conditions, is shown on [Send a parcel](/send-parcel#rules) before you book. Please check it every time: it can change.

### How is the price worked out?
You get an estimate when you book. We weigh the parcel at the drop-off point, rounding up to the next 0.5 kg, and that sets the final price, which you pay before the parcel leaves.

### How do I follow my parcel?
Every parcel has a tracking number. Open [My shipments](/shipments) or enter the number on [Track a parcel](/track).

### Can I cancel a parcel?
Yes, until it leaves the drop-off point. If you've already paid, you get a refund. See [Sending parcels](/terms#parcels).

## Selling on Phloem {#selling}

### How do I open a shop?
Go to [Become a seller](/sell). You'll need your date of birth and home address in your profile. We review every new shop before it goes live and may ask for documents such as a food hygiene certificate.

### How and when am I paid?
Phloem keeps a commission on the dishes of each delivered order; the delivery fee is yours in full. Your earnings are added to your balance when an order is marked delivered. See [Selling on Phloem](/terms#sellers).

## Your account and data {#account}

### I forgot my password
On the log in page, choose **Forgot password?** and we'll email you a link to set a new one.

### How do I keep my account safe?
Use a password you don't use anywhere else, and turn on [Two-step sign-in](/account/two-factor) so a code from your phone is needed when you log in.

### What do you do with my data?
We only collect what we need to run your orders, shop and parcels. See [What we collect](/privacy#what) and [Who sees what](/privacy#who) in our Privacy Policy.

### How do I delete my account?
Email **support@phloem.app** from your account's email address and we'll delete it. Some records, such as paid orders, must be kept for a time by law. See [Your rights](/privacy#rights).
